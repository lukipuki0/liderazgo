# Healthy aging papers: local index

Eleven PDFs are in this folder and were revalidated locally (see `validation-report.json`). Five come from the original 13-paper selection. Six are complementary additions. Eight original papers and five additions were not downloaded. No further downloads were attempted for this index.

Semantic or topic selection is not a certified exact match to a Scopus Boolean query. Additions can be complementary; they were not part of the original 13. Do not treat every paper as a study of middle age only. Several records cover middle-aged and older adults together, life-course nutrition, or broader adult samples.

Journals below are Europe PMC `journal.title` values from `search-metadata.json` or `additional-search-metadata.json`. They are not inferred from the publisher URL. PDF page counts are from the download records and matched pypdf on revalidation. Citation pages are Europe PMC `pageInfo` (article numbers when that is what the record stores).

## Bibliographic notes

- Paper 9: journal issue year 2022 (Nutrition reviews, 2022 Apr). Europe PMC first index date is 2021-08-17, which is the basis for the online-2021 versus issue-2022 note. The same record's `firstPublicationDate` is 2022-04-01. Confirm the publisher online date before citing 2021 as the publication year.
- Paper 12: Europe PMC journal year is 2026 (issue date 2026 Jan; electronic publication date on that record 2025-12-22). The initial Consensus result reported 2025. Confirm the publisher record before choosing a citation year.
- Addition 14 (WHAP): no significant independent plant-food/cognition association in the fully adjusted model. Not a causal benefit claim.
- Local validation: pypdf 6.18.1 opened each of the 11 PDFs; each had nonzero pages matching the download record; the first two pages contained the normalized title and DOI; SHA-256 matched the result record. Verdicts are all pass. Details are in `validation-report.json`.
